Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

LMS Bot is an internal tool used exclusively by Live Moving & Storage staff. It connects to QuickBooks Online to create invoices and payment links.

Data collected

The app accesses QuickBooks Online customer and invoice data solely to fulfill requests made by authorized internal users via Telegram. No data is stored beyond what QuickBooks Online retains natively.

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. This app is not available to the public.

Contact

For questions, contact the app administrator.
